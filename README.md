🎂 Cake & Create: Your Digital Bakery
Where sweetness meets creativity. Cake & Create is a full-stack web application designed to bridge the gap between classic bakeries and modern customization. Whether you're looking for a quick gourmet fix or want to design the centerpiece for your next big event, our platform makes the process as seamless as a layer of buttercream.

✨ Key Features

🎨 Custom Cake Builder: Don't just buy a cake—create it. Choose your base, frosting, and decorations with our interactive customization tool.

🛒 Ready-to-Ship Shop: Browse our curated collection of signature cakes, ready for instant purchase and delivery.

⭐ Community Ratings: Shop with confidence. View detailed ratings and reviews from other cake lovers for every item in our inventory.

🚚 Order & Delivery History: Keep track of your sweet journey. Access a full history of your past orders and monitor real-time delivery status.

🥧 Interactive UI: A modern, visually-driven interface designed to showcase high-quality bakery products and simplify the checkout process.

🛠️ How It Works

Browse or Build: Choose from our pre-made menu or enter the "Creator Mode" to build a cake from scratch.

Secure Checkout: Add your selection to the cart and pay via our secure payment gateway.

Track: Check your delivery history to see when your treats will arrive at your door.

Rate: After enjoying your cake, leave a rating to help the community find the best flavors!
